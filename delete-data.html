Delete your data

Pond Patrol is made by Rolle Soikkeli. The game has no accounts of its own, and we (the developer) don't store any data about you on any server. Here's how to delete everything the game keeps.

1. Your progress on the phone

Your coins, unlocks, styles and settings are saved only on your device.

  • In the game: Settings > Reset progress (tap it twice to confirm) erases it, or
  • uninstall Pond Patrol: that deletes it from the device too.

2. Google Play Games data (only if you signed in)

If you chose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google keeps your Dark Tower leaderboard score, your achievements and a cloud copy of your progress (Saved Games) with your Play Games profile. To delete them:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or Play Games in the Play Store app).
  2. Go to your profile > Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Play Games account & data, then Delete individual game data and pick Pond Patrol (or delete your whole Play Games account).

This removes Pond Patrol's scores, achievements and saved game. Google may keep some data for a limited time under Google's privacy policy. To stop the game sending anything new, switch Settings > Play Games off in the game.
