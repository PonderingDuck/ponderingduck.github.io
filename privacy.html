Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Pond Patrol ("the game") is made by Rolle Soikkeli ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. Contact: rolle.soikkeli@outlook.com.

What we collect

We don't collect any personal data ourselves. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress (coins, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back this save up to your own Google account, so a new phone can restore it. We never see that backup.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example at the Wishing Well or to double your coins after a run. It shows no banner or pop-up ads. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and basic device information
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data (for example, whether an ad loaded or was watched)

Google uses this data to serve ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and produce statistics. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. The Personalised ads switch in Settings is off by default; while it's off, ads you choose to watch are requested as non-personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads in the game are limited to general-audience (rated "G") content.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress from the device. Settings > Reset progress erases it without uninstalling. We hold no other data about you to delete. For ad data, use Google's tools above or your device's ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
