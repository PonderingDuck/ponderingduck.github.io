Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Pond Patrol ("the game") is made by Rolle Soikkeli ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. Contact: rolle.soikkeli@outlook.com.

What we collect

We don't collect any personal data ourselves. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress (coins, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back this save up to your own Google account, so a new phone can restore it. We never see that backup.

No ads, no tracking, no internet

The game has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it doesn't connect to the internet. It contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. (The Privacy policy button in Settings simply opens this page in your web browser.)

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress from the device. Settings > Reset progress erases it without uninstalling. We hold no other data about you to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email rolle.soikkeli@outlook.com.
