Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 (Google Play Games added)

Pond Patrol ("the game") is made by Rolle Soikkeli ("we"). This policy explains what data the game handles and why. Contact: rolle.soikkeli@outlook.com.

What we collect

We don't collect any personal data ourselves. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress (coins, unlocks, settings) is saved only on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back this save up to your own Google account, so a new phone can restore it. We never see that backup.

Optional: Google Play Games

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games (the game asks once, and you can switch it in Settings > Play Games), the game uses Google Play Games Services to show the Dark Tower leaderboard, unlock achievements and keep a copy of your progress in your Google account (Saved Games), so a new phone can continue where the old one left off. For this, Google processes your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), your scores, achievements and saved game, under Google's privacy policy. This is the only time the game connects to the internet. If you don't sign in, the game stays fully offline and nothing leaves your device. You can see, manage or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account.

No ads, no tracking

The game has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it contains no SDKs that track you. (The Privacy policy button in Settings simply opens this page in your web browser.)

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your saved progress from the device. Settings > Reset progress erases it without uninstalling. We hold no other data about you to delete; your Play Games data (if you signed in) is managed in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
